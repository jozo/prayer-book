A Catholic prayer book in your pocket. Basic prayers, the rosary, litanies, prayers to the saints and more, sorted into categories and easy to find.

Features:
🔍 Search across all prayers (accents don't matter)
🔖 Bookmark your favorite prayers
📋 Create your own lists, e.g. for morning or evening prayers
✍️ Add your own prayers, and back them up or move them to another phone
🌍 Switch a prayer to another language
🌙 Light and dark theme

Works completely offline. No ads, no tracking, no account. Free and open source.

Prayers are available in English, French, Italian, Latin, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Slovak, Spanish and Swahili. Not every prayer is available in every language.

Missing a prayer or found a mistake? Send it through "Feedback & Ideas" in the app.
